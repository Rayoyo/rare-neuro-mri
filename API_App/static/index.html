    Model

    Values reported by the service at startup, read from the artifacts produced during training.

    Expected Calibration Error measures how far the reported confidence sits from the observed accuracy. Below roughly 0.02 the probabilities can be read at face value.

    The confidence threshold is the 5th percentile of the confidences measured on the validation set: it declines about 5 % of genuine inputs by construction, which is the cost of being able to refuse an image that is not one of the five conditions.

    Conditions

    The five classes the model was trained on, in the order it predicts them.

    Condition ORPHA code Description
    Fukuyama Muscular Dystrophy ORPHA:272 Congenital muscular dystrophy with severe cortical brain malformations (cobblestone lissencephaly).
    Hallervorden Spatz Disease ORPHA:157850 Neurodegenerative movement disorder characterized by progressive iron accumulation in the basal ganglia (PKAN).
    Moyamoya Disease ORPHA:2573 Progressive cerebrovascular disorder with internal carotid artery stenosis and collateral blood vessel formation.
    Pachygyria Cerebellar Hypoplasia ORPHA:2524 Malformation of cortical development with abnormally thick gyri paired with cerebellar underdevelopment.
    Walker Warburg Syndrome ORPHA:899 Severe congenital muscular dystrophy with complex structural brain and eye abnormalities.

    Walker-Warburg syndrome and Fukuyama muscular dystrophy are both congenital muscular dystrophies with overlapping MRI appearance, so confusion between them is clinically plausible.

    About

    This service classifies a single axial brain MRI image into one of five rare neurological conditions. It runs a ConvNeXt-Tiny network, pre-trained on ImageNet and fine-tuned on 1 400 curated images.

    The model has exactly five outputs and always returns one of them, so an image that is not one of these conditions would still receive a confident-looking answer. To limit that, predictions whose confidence falls below the threshold are marked outside the training distribution and should be ignored.

    The interface calls the same public endpoints available to any client: /predict, /gradcam, /model-info, /classes and /health. Full interactive documentation is at /docs, and the OpenAPI schema at /openapi.json.

    Research demonstrator trained on a SMALL curated dataset. Not a medical device; not for diagnostic use.
